Frequently Asked

What is Makhana? ▼

Makhana, also known as fox nuts or lotus seeds, is a superfood that has been part of Indian cuisine for centuries. It's rich in protein, fiber, and low in calories.

Is it suitable for weight loss? ▼

Yes! At only 130 calories per serving with 8g of fiber, makhana is perfect for weight management. It keeps you full and satisfied without guilt.

How long does it stay fresh? ▼

Our makhana stays fresh for 6 months when stored in an airtight container at room temperature. Our packaging is designed to maintain optimal freshness.
